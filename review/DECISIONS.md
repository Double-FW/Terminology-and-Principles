## D01 — Satisfactory, Satisfaction and POURS

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Proposed terminology resolution only; not blanket approval of all glossary entry wording or source frameworks.

**Observed wording or risk:** The current POURS guide uses Satisfactory for the additional principle. The Little Book also contains a source titled POUR + S (Satisfaction). This is an observed naming variation; it does not establish that both sources have identical scope.

**Recorded resolution:** Use Satisfactory for the proposed principle and satisfaction for the experience assessment. Preserve historical titles; add scoped explanations rather than silently renaming source passages.

## D02 — Comparable and equivalent experience

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Proposed terminology resolution only; not blanket approval of all glossary entry wording or source frameworks.

**Observed wording or risk:** The Little Book governance map uses comparable experience. Synchronistic Design explains equivalent experience in terms of preserved meaning and value. Both reject identical interfaces as the objective; their use substantially overlaps.

**Recorded resolution:** Keep both terms and require a stated comparison across outcomes, effort, privacy, availability, control and dignity. Do not invent an absolute universal distinction or replace every occurrence.

## D05 — ACT rules, ACT-style procedures and user scripts

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Proposed terminology resolution only; not blanket approval of all glossary entry wording or source frameworks.

**Observed wording or risk:** QA supplies a locally structured ACT-style test system, while programme guidance calls for user ACT-style scripts. The W3C ACT work has its own rules format and rule status. These labels must not imply equal scope or external approval.

## D06 — Robustness, reliability and Feeling Safe

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Agentic Robustness definition text only. LGIR, detailed guidelines, level labels and editorial notes remain framework proposals, not validated requirements.

**Observed wording or risk:** The AI Act delivery guide explicitly distinguishes AI Act robustness from WCAG robustness. POURS uses Feeling Safe as an emotional lens with a separate warning that reassurance does not establish security.

**Recorded resolution:** Adopt the owner-supplied Agentic Robustness definition as a distinct Double-FW definition, retaining WCAG robustness as its separate source-owned term.

**Owner-supplied definition:** Agentic systems, their underlying data, and their human-facing interfaces must be resilient, transparent, and interoperable enough to operate reliably across diverse models, shifting contexts, and human oversight mechanisms, both now and as capabilities evolve.

The original proposal to merely qualify the domain is retained in the structured history. The response now supplies the separate definition and a new framework proposal.

## D07 — Evidence, status and human authority

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Proposed terminology resolution only; not blanket approval of all glossary entry wording or source frameworks.

**Observed wording or risk:** The policy guidance separates applicable duties, approved commitments, verified practice and proposals. QA and maturity examples also distinguish actual evidence from prepared or fictional records. Source words such as validated and approved need a stated object and scope.

**Recorded resolution:** Keep observation, estimate, hypothesis, synthetic example, proposal and approval distinct. Preserve unknown, not tested and not applicable as different states. Never infer approval from a template, Git merge or a new export date.

## D08 — Double-FW meanings and externally owned requirements

**Status:** Approved by Gareth Ford Williams on 20 November 2026. **Scope:** Proposed terminology resolution only; not blanket approval of all glossary entry wording or source frameworks.
