## Accessibility

**Reference:** DFW-T0001 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

Participation by disabled people through services, environments, information and interactions that address relevant barriers.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** Do not use a standards pass or a claim about an average user as proof of access for everyone.

**Example:** We tested access to the complete booking journey with the relevant technologies and people.

## Inclusion

**Reference:** DFW-T0002 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

## Usability

**Reference:** DFW-T0003 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

How effectively people can achieve a specified purpose, with appropriate effort and experience in their actual context.

## Inclusive design

**Reference:** DFW-T0004 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

A design practice that investigates differences in people and circumstances and addresses exclusion through participation and evidence.

## Comparable experience

**Reference:** DFW-T0006 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

A fair opportunity to achieve the intended outcome with appropriate quality, control, dignity and effort across different ways of interacting.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** Comparable does not require identical interfaces or times; it does require explicit comparison of material differences.

**Example:** Compare privacy, effort, availability and success across online and supported routes.

**Applicable approved terminology resolution:** D02. This does not approve all entry wording. [Decision register](../review/DECISIONS.md).

## Equivalent experience

**Reference:** DFW-T0007 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

Preservation of the meaningful value of an experience across different formats, interactions, technologies and service routes.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** An alternative route is not equivalent merely because it reaches the same administrative endpoint.

## Non-apparent disability

**Reference:** DFW-T0018 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

A context-dependent description of disability that is not readily noticeable in the interaction being considered.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.
