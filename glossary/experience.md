## Customer experience (CX)

**Reference:** DFW-T0022 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

## Satisfactory

**Reference:** DFW-T0024 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** double fw framework

The proposed additional Double-FW experience principle: meeting needs and reasonable expectations while preserving dignity, identity and control.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** It is not a fifth official WCAG principle; favourable ratings cannot cancel access failures.

**Example:** Evaluate correct completion, effort, choice and dignity alongside the person's account.
