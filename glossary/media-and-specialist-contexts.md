## Audio description (AD)

**Reference:** DFW-T0100 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

An access route that communicates relevant visual information through spoken description integrated with the programme experience.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** A separate track is not always necessary; evaluate what the finished soundtrack communicates and the applicable requirements.

**Example:** Describe a silent visual event needed to follow the story while preserving important sound.

**Also encountered as:** AD

**Source context:**

## Captions

**Reference:** DFW-T0101 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

Synchronised text conveying relevant speech and meaningful non-speech audio, including speaker information where needed.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.

**Distinctions and limits:** A transcript or dialogue-only translation may not provide the same access.

## Transcript

**Reference:** DFW-T0103 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

A text representation of relevant spoken or media content, with the needed structure and identification.

## Extended reality (XR)

**Reference:** DFW-T0104 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** source grounded synthesis

An umbrella term for relevant immersive or augmented experiences, with the specific technology and interaction scope stated.

**Scope:** Working Double-FW cross-repository terminology; preserve the owning source context.
