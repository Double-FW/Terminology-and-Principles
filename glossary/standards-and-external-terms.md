## WCAG

**Reference:** DFW-T0147 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** external paraphrase

W3C guidance containing testable success criteria for web-content accessibility.

**Scope:** Brief orientation only; the originating organisation owns the authoritative meaning.

**Distinctions and limits:** Record the edition and level; do not infer legal applicability from the acronym.

**Example:** Identify WCAG 2.2 and the selected level in the evaluation scope.

**Applicable approved terminology resolution:** D08. This does not approve all entry wording. [Decision register](../review/DECISIONS.md).

**Source context:**

- [External primary reference](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

## POUR

**Reference:** DFW-T0148 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** external paraphrase

The four organising WCAG principles: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable and Robust.

**Scope:** Brief orientation only; the originating organisation owns the authoritative meaning.

**Distinctions and limits:** POURS is the separate Double-FW extension; keep their provenance clear.

**Example:** Use POUR when explaining the four official WCAG principles.

**Applicable approved terminology resolution:** D08. This does not approve all entry wording. [Decision register](../review/DECISIONS.md).

**Source context:**

- [External primary reference](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/intro)

## Robust (WCAG)

**Reference:** DFW-T0152 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** external paraphrase

Content supports reliable interpretation by different user agents, including assistive technology.

**Scope:** Brief orientation only; the originating organisation owns the authoritative meaning.

**Distinctions and limits:** Do not conflate WCAG compatibility with every meaning of reliability, AI robustness or cybersecurity.

**Example:** Record tested technology combinations and remaining compatibility gaps.

## Conformance

**Reference:** DFW-T0153 · **Status:** Proposed for owner review · **Basis:** external paraphrase

Meeting the applicable requirements of the identified standard or specification.

**Scope:** Brief orientation only; the originating organisation owns the authoritative meaning.

**Distinctions and limits:** Specify the target and scope; a sample pass does not establish every conformance condition.
