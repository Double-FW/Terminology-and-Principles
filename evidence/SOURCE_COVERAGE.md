## Reproducibility

[source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json) identifies commits, paths, Git blob hashes and SHA-256 hashes for the scanned and excluded Markdown sources. [term-inventory.csv](term-inventory.csv) gives literal document/repository counts; [term-inventory.json](term-inventory.json) adds located example contexts. [primary-references.json](primary-references.json) records external retrieval results, including unsuccessful legal-page retrievals.

A literal match is not a definition, approval or agreement. The scanner records the first matching line per document and at most six sample contexts per term. It does not find every synonym, morphological variant or implied concept. An unmatched preferred label can still be an editorial synthesis supported by differently worded source context.

## Exclusions and limits

Path-based exclusions: any path containing rag, archive or historical; a Sources directory; CHANGELOG.md; huggingface/ setup material; and VALIDATION_REPORT.md. Some excluded material can be useful historical evidence, so selected supporting sources may be added explicitly. Other duplicate prose or historical material can remain in the scanned set. Counts are discovery aids, not a measure of independent evidence.

The snapshot was checked against main at collection, not continually monitored. Source identity checks do not validate scientific claims, fulfil legal review, resolve onward broken links or confirm Hugging Face publication. No independent domain review or participant study was performed.

The three EUR-Lex locators returned verification pages, not substantive legal text. They are recorded as candidate locators with retrieval limitations, not as inspected legal authorities for new definitions.
