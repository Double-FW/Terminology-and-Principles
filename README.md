## Status and authority

Seven approved resolutions establish terminology direction. D06 adopts the exact supplied Agentic Robustness definition, scoped to its definition text. Other individual wording remains proposed. LGIR, its provisions, A/AA labels and editorial notes are proposals, not a validated standard or deployed system.

Use Double-FW meanings for Double-FW methods within their adopted scope. External terms retain their originating authority. Neither the glossary nor LGIR amends WCAG, determines legal applicability, adopts client policy or certifies a product. Preserve context, source identifiers, qualifications and actual decision authority.
