# Propose a terminology change

You do not need to write code to suggest a change. Describe:

1. The term, its glossary reference if known, and the document where it caused difficulty.
2. The intended meaning and a plain-English example.
3. What could be misunderstood, and who or what could be affected.
4. The public source and the specific passage supporting the suggestion.
5. Whether this is a spelling change, clarification, new meaning, disputed interpretation or proposed convention.

The maintainer should inspect the owning sources and update the structured record and generated pages together. Preserve contrary evidence and source status. Review changes to terms used in law, standards, statistics, identity or decisions with the relevant expertise.

Before approval, complete a decision record containing the change, reason, sources, alternatives, scope, affected entries and documents, actual approver, date and review trigger. Leave approver/date empty until a real decision is made. Approval must be scoped: accepting wording does not validate an entire framework.

No contributor should add private source contents, personal data, credentials or invented citations. Public links do not by themselves grant reuse rights. See [NOTICE](NOTICE.md).
